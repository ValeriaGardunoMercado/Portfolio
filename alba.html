ALBA, a portable breast cancer screening device

The Story

What happened

ALBA is a portable device that screens for breast anomalies without radiation or discomfort. I led a team of five. I designed the shape of the sensor, ran the first tests in MATLAB (including the interface and reconstructions with different meshes), and then helped build the C# interface: the buttons, the graphs, the reconstructed image, and the path where data is stored.

The hardest part of the interface was showing the reconstructed image: a grid with a label for each electrode around it.

Days before a competition, the device showed the anomaly at the wrong electrode. I redid reconstructions with data that had worked before, which ruled out the software. I asked the team to check the instrument that takes the measurements and the multiplexer that controls the patterns. They found unsoldered pins, fixed them, tested again, and we went to the competition. ALBA won first place in the biomedical category, and first place in the local phase of InnovaTec.
