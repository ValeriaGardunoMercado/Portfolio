Mechatronics engineer driven by complex engineering challenges and end-to-end hardware-software systems.

Hi! I'm Valeria, a Mechatronics Engineering student who loves turning complex engineering concepts into high-performance technological solutions.

My professional focus centers on embedded systems design, specialization in control systems, robust electronic circuit design, and efficient software development (using C++, Python, and C#) for automation, hardware control, and data acquisition.

I am strongly driven by the automotive industry, a field that fascinates me due to its high standards and continuous innovation. I thoroughly enjoy the full engineering lifecycle, with a keen focus on testing, validation, and quality assurance for firmware and electronic systems—ensuring every solution operates with maximum precision and reliability.

I am always looking to collaborate on challenging projects that push the boundaries of engineering and vehicular technology. Let's connect!
