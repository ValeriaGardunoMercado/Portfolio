The Story

What happened

The platform uses a polyglot architecture: several database technologies working together, each one for the kind of data it handles best. It combines MongoDB and PostgreSQL with pgSphere and follows IVOA standards, together with FAIR principles for the data.

I worked on verifying the design of the hybrid architecture. I also prepared the presentation script and slides covering the objectives, the methodology and the conclusions of the project.
